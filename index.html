    How to Play

    1. Choose a difficulty and a fleet composition, then press Start Game.
    2. On the placement screen, click a ship in the tray to select it, then click a board cell to drop it — the clicked cell is the ship's top-left anchor. Press R or the Rotate button to switch between horizontal and vertical. Click a placed ship to pick it up again.
    3. Placements that leave the grid or overlap another ship are refused; the preview turns red. Use Randomize Placement to fill the rest of the fleet.
    4. In battle, click a cell on the larger enemy board to fire. Hits are red, misses are grey and sunk ships are marked. The enemy fires back after each of your shots.
    5. Sink every enemy ship to win. The live feed and Move History record every shot.
